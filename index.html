Our Story

Born From a Love of Celebrations

Aapke Business Ka Naam started with a simple belief — that every celebration, big or small, deserves to feel magical. What began as decorating birthdays for family and friends has grown into a full-service studio trusted for weddings, baby showers, anniversaries and everything in between.

Today, our decorators, designers and setup crew work as one team on your special day — combining creativity with the kind of punctuality your guests will actually notice.

Our Mission

To make stress-free, beautifully crafted celebrations accessible to every family — with honest pricing and dependable service.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted decoration studio in every city we serve — known equally for creativity and reliability.
